2) Processed data
Direct personal data:
- User account email address
- Phone number (international country code + number) if provided by the user

Other functional service data:
- Free nickname entered by the user
- Collaborative content (trips, participants, messages, expenses, activities,
  shopping, meals, practical information)
- Shared attachments and photos
- Technical data required for operation and security

3) Purpose and legal basis
- Service execution: authentication, account management, collaborative app
  features
- Profile contact setting: storing the phone number so it can be used only
  according to the sharing preferences explicitly configured by the user
- Legitimate interest: security, abuse prevention, operational continuity
- Consent: notifications according to permissions granted by the user on their
  device/browser

Phone number visibility:
- By default, the phone number is not visible to any other user
- The phone number is shown only if the user explicitly enables a sharing
  setting for a trip context

4) Location and subprocessors
Data is hosted in Europe (europe-west9 region) and subject to European law.
The website is delivered through Vercel's global CDN infrastructure (no single
static hosting region).

Technical subprocessors:
- Vercel (web hosting)
- Google Firebase: Authentication, Firestore, Storage, Functions, FCM
- Google (Gemini API): artificial intelligence features (see section 5)

5) Artificial intelligence features
Some optional features rely on Google's Gemini API. Data is sent to it only
when the user triggers one of these features:
- Creating an activity from a free-text description: the text entered, the
  device date and time, and the trip title, destination and dates
- Generating activities from documents: the documents selected in "My
  documents" (files and scanned code content), the instructions entered, and
  the trip title, destination and dates
- Shopping list consolidation: the labels and quantities of the items and of
  the meal ingredients
- Recipe generation: the recipe name and the number of servings

Legal basis: service execution, at the user's request.
AI responses are suggestions: nothing is saved to the trip without the user's
confirmation.
Under Google's terms for the paid services of the Gemini API, this data is not
used to improve Google's products; Google may retain it for a limited period
to detect abuse and meet its legal obligations. This processing may take place
outside the European Union.

Voice dictation: when the user dictates a description, their voice is turned
into text by the speech recognition service of their browser or device (for
example Google or Apple), under that provider's terms. The application only
receives and stores the resulting text.

6) Retention period
- Account and content data: retained while the account is active
- Deletion/anonymization after validated request: within 30 days
- Technical backups: purged within 90 days maximum
- Security/anti-abuse data: limited and proportionate retention
  (12 months maximum)
